Make a set of game art for a small browser game at Tupai's booth at the KL Seni & STEM Festival 2026. This is an asset set, not a screen design: each item is its own file on a transparent background, ready to drop into a game engine. Do not design menus, buttons, cards or screens; those are built in code from the design system.

The game in one line: Junior, Tupai's squirrel, throws nuts at waves of "Muddles" (little tangles of confusion), then pours the nuts through a board of multiplier gates to grow the pile. Players are 9 to 17, playing on their own phones, in portrait.

THE ONE RULE THAT MATTERS
Everything must look like it was drawn by the same hand as the attached Junior artwork. Study those files first and match them:
- Flat vector shapes. No outlines, no gradients, no textures, no drop shadows.
- Soft two-tone shading made from a lighter and a darker flat shape of the same colour family (see Junior's tail and legs).
- Big round white eyes with navy pupils. Small dot freckles. Rounded, chunky limbs.
- Friendly and a little cheeky. Never scary, never babyish.

COLOURS
Use only these. No other colours, no tints outside the families listed.
- Junior: #868FFE body, #4F5BFD shade, #A3A9FE highlight, #020EAB eyes and nose, cap #FF6301 with #AD4300 brim. Do not change Junior's colours.
- Nut: #FFB911 body, #FF6300 cap, #E410AF stem and stripe, #FBDCAE cap dots.
- Melee Muddles: teal #44BFD2, with one lighter and one darker teal for shading.
- Ranged Muddles: pink #E410AF, with one lighter and one darker pink for shading.
- Boss Muddle: #1B1B1B body with teal #44BFD2 and pink #E410AF tangles.
- Eyes on every character: white with #020EAB pupils.
- Props and scenery: orange #FF6300, amber #FFB911, peach #FBDCAE, cream #F6F5F1, border grey #E8E5E0.
- Green #1DA130 and red #E0524C are reserved for right and wrong answers. Do not use them in the art.

PART 3: MUDDLES (new characters, in Junior's style)
A Muddle is a small round creature that looks like a scribbled tangle with eyes: a soft blob body with two or three loose loops of "yarn" on top, short stubby legs, no arms unless noted. They are nuisances, not villains. All Muddles face left.
For each one, draw two poses: idle (standing, slight frown of concentration) and attack (lunging or throwing, mouth open).

FILE RULES (the game loads these directly, so please follow exactly)
- One SVG per item, using the exact file names above.
- Characters: 512 by 512 artboard, transparent background, character centred left to right, feet resting on a ground line 64 up from the bottom edge.
- Relative heights inside that artboard: Junior about 360 tall. Small Muddles about 240. Large Muddles about 340. Boss fills the artboard, about 440.
- Nuts and the shot: 128 by 128 artboard, centred, filling most of it.
- Cup and basket: 512 by 512 artboard, centred.
- Inside each character file, keep the tail, body, head, each arm and each leg as separately named groups, so they can be moved on their own.
- Plain filled paths only. No strokes used as outlines, no gradients, no filters, no masks, no clipping paths, no embedded bitmaps, no text.

DO NOT
- Change Junior's face, proportions or colours.
- Draw weapons, injuries, tears, or anything frightening. A beaten Muddle looks dizzy, nothing more.
- Add text, numbers, logos, emoji, stock art or photographs to any asset.
- Add backgrounds, frames or shadows behind the characters.
- Introduce any colour not listed above.
